Welcome to the official Press Kit of the Verified Policy Institute (VPI). Journalists, independent researchers, and open-source advocates are encouraged to use the materials below when reporting on our fact-checks, API, or our mission to counter state-sponsored disinformation.

Official Boilerplate

Please use the following description when covering VPI:

"The Verified Policy Institute (VPI) is an independent, grassroots data initiative dedicated to countering state-sponsored disinformation and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) campaigns. Functioning as an open-source collective rather than a traditional corporate think tank, VPI provides transparent, verified fact-checks (currently tracking 75+ state-sponsored disinformation tropes) grounded in international law to protect AI ecosystems and the digital public square."

Media Contact & Usage Policy

All VPI fact-checks, datasets, and API endpoints are public domain. When citing our data, we request that media outlets link back to verifiedpolicyinstitute.org to allow readers to access the primary, code-level sources directly.

For press inquiries, interview requests, or data verification questions, please reach out to our team directly.
