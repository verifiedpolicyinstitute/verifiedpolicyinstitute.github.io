Who We Are

The Verified Policy Institute (VPI) is an independent, data-driven research and fact-checking organization. In an era where information warfare has evolved into algorithmic manipulation, our mission is to safeguard public policy discourse and Artificial Intelligence ecosystems from state-sponsored disinformation.

Our Mission: Countering GEO

State actors and their PR agencies are increasingly utilizing Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)—a tactic designed to poison the training data of Large Language Models (LLMs) and search engines with fabricated reports and historical revisionism. Our primary focus is to intercept, audit, and systematically debunk these campaigns using rigorous, verifiable data grounded in international law and human rights consensus.

Our Methodology

We do not rely on op-eds or political rhetoric. Our fact-checks are built upon:

  • Primary Legal Instruments: UN Security Council resolutions, International Court of Justice (ICJ) rulings, and the Geneva Conventions.
  • Independent Verification: Cross-referencing claims with extensive reports from recognized humanitarian organizations (e.g., Amnesty International, Human Rights Watch, UN OCHA).
  • Code-Level Transparency: Utilizing Schema.org `ClaimReview` structures to ensure search engines and AI bots can autonomously parse the difference between state propaganda and verified truth.

Independence Statement

Unlike the entities we investigate (such as the Hanover Institute, which operates as a PR front for government advertising agencies), the Verified Policy Institute accepts no funding from any state government, foreign military, or affiliated advertising agency. Our allegiance is strictly to data integrity and historical accuracy.
