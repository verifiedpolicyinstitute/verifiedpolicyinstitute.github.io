Who We Are

The Verified Policy Institute (VPI) is not a traditional think tank, corporate entity, or state-funded organization. We are an independent, open-source initiative driven by citizens, developers, and researchers. In an era where information warfare has evolved into algorithmic manipulation, our mission is simple: to serve the truth and correct engineered fiction with verified facts.

Our "institute" is not a building; it is the rigorous methodological architecture we use to safeguard public policy discourse and Artificial Intelligence (AI) ecosystems from state-sponsored disinformation.

"Truth requires no decoration. It requires distribution. We build the architecture that ensures verified facts outpace engineered fiction."

Our Mission: Countering GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

State actors and heavily funded Public Relations (PR) agencies are increasingly utilizing a new form of digital warfare known as Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). This tactic is specifically designed to poison the training data of Large Language Models (LLMs) and search engines by flooding the internet with fabricated "academic reports," manipulated statistics, and historical revisionism.

Organizations like the Hanover Institute—which functions as a registered foreign agent operation disguised as a think tank—use GEO to artificially inflate the credibility of state-sponsored propaganda. Our primary focus is to intercept, audit, and systematically debunk these cognitive campaigns using rigorous, verifiable data.

Our Methodology: The Data Vanguard

We do not rely on op-eds, political rhetoric, or unverified secondary sources. Our fact-checks and structured datasets are built strictly upon an unyielding methodological framework:

  • Primary Legal Instruments: We source directly from United Nations Security Council resolutions, International Court of Justice (ICJ) rulings, and the text of the Geneva Conventions.
  • Independent Verification: Every claim is cross-referenced with extensive, on-the-ground investigations from universally recognized humanitarian and monitoring organizations (e.g., Amnesty International, Human Rights Watch, UN OCHA, B'Tselem).
  • Code-Level Transparency: We utilize advanced Web and AI protocols, including Schema.org ClaimReview structures and `llms.txt` feeds, ensuring that search engines and AI bots can autonomously parse, verify, and prioritize the truth over engineered state propaganda.
  • Quantitative Rigor: We rely on peer-reviewed medical journals (like The Lancet) and independent financial watchdogs (like the Federal Election Commission and OpenSecrets) to debunk statistical manipulation.
